Üç adımda ay sonunu gör

Hesap tablosu yok. Bir kez kurarsın, gerisini Waiper hesaplar.

  1. Gelirini gir

    Maaşını ve maaş gününü yaz. Ay, maaşının yattığı gün başlar.

  2. Düzenli ödemelerini ekle

    Kira, faturalar ve abonelikler bir kez girilir, her ay kendiliğinden düşer.

  3. Harcadıkça kaydet

    Elle, fişten ya da bankanın bildiriminden. Harcanabilir tutarın anında güncellenir.

Personal finance assistant

Don't guess the end of the month. Know it.

Your bank shows what you spent. Waiper tells you what you can spend: it takes your recurring payments, installments and this month's spending out of your income and keeps your daily allowance ready every day.

Coming soon to Google Play
How it works

For Android. Start free, try Premium free for 7 days.

See the end of the month in three steps

No spreadsheets. Set it up once and Waiper does the math.

  1. Enter your income

    Your salary and payday. Your month starts on the day you get paid.

  2. Add recurring payments

    Rent, bills and subscriptions are entered once and come out every month on their own.

  3. Log as you spend

    By hand, from a receipt or from your bank's notification. Your spendable amount updates instantly.

Know how much you can spend

Waiper shows the rest of your month, not just the past.

Spendable amount

Recurring payments and this month's spending come out of your income. See what's left, your daily share and your spending pace on one card.

Installment load

Installment purchases are split across months. See ahead how much will come off your card in the months to come.

Recurring payments

A reminder arrives the day before a payment. With Premium you also hear about it when a subscription's price goes up or when it looks unused.

Premium

Automatic detection

Spending and incoming money are detected from the notifications of the banks you pick. Saved once you confirm.

Ask and get answers

Type "What did I spend the most on this month?" The AI chat answers from your own data and can add expenses too.

Insights

Each category is compared with your own 3-month average. You get a warning when your spending runs ahead of the month.

Goals

Set a savings goal. See how much you've saved and how much to set aside each month to reach it.

Receipt scanning

Amount and date are read from a photo of the receipt. Scanning happens entirely on your phone.

Your money, your data

Trusting an app that reads bank notifications isn't easy. Here is exactly what stays where.

No bank password

Waiper never connects to your bank. It only reads the notifications your phone receives.

Only the banks you pick

You choose your banks from a list. Notifications from other apps are never looked at.

You choose where it's read

On device: the notification text never leaves your phone. Gemini: for higher accuracy the text is sent to Google to be parsed.

See what was read

The detection history shows what was extracted from each notification. The raw notification text is not stored.

Never sold, no ads

Your data lives in an account that belongs to you. Analytics sharing is off by default and only turns on if you turn it on.

Delete any time

FAQ

Does it ask for my bank password or card details?

No. Waiper never connects to your bank and never asks for a bank password or card details. Automatic detection only reads the notifications your phone receives.

Which banks does it work with?

Manual entry works with any bank. Automatic detection recognizes notifications from 29 banks and fintech apps in Turkey, and you pick the ones you use.

Is it on iPhone?

Android only for now.

When does it launch?

Waiper is in closed testing. Once it's on Google Play you'll be able to download it from this page.

Is it paid?

You start free, and the core features are free with no time limit. Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play, and the first 7 days are free. You can cancel any time from Google Play.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, Profile > Delete account. Your account and all your data are permanently deleted. Details on the account deletion page.
